Workshop Studio is provided by AWS to vend a temporary account for you to complete this workshop. While there are some limitations and boundaries on this account, once you've completed these labs, you're encouraged to explore and experiment further with Amazon OpenSearch Service.

To access your account, complete the following steps.

1. Navigate to [Workshop Studio](https://catalog.us-east-1.prod.workshops.aws/join)  or use the link provided by your facilitator.

2. Click on **Email one-time password**.

   :image[SignIn]{src="/static/signin.png"}

3. Enter your email address and click Send passcode.

   :image[onetimepasscode]{src="/static/onetimepasscode.png"}

4. This will send an email to your nominated account with a one-time password. Copy the code from the email to enter in the next step.
  
:::alert{header="Important" type="warning"}
Troubleshooting
If you don't receive an email, please check your spam folder. Alternatively, please check with your event facilitator to ensure your email has been whitelisted.

:::
    
5. Enter the passcode from the email under **Event access code** and click **Sign in.**

   :image[eventaccesscode]{src="/static/eventcode.png"}

6. Once signed in, enter the event code provided by your facilitator and click **Next.** If you used a link provided by your facilitator that included an event code, you can skip this step

   :image[onetimecode]{src="/static/onetimepasscode-code.png"}

7. Review the Terms and Conditions, mark the checkbox next to **I agree with the Terms and Conditions** and click **Join event.**
   
8. You'll see the steps for the workshop on the left. To access your AWS account, click **Open AWS console** in the bottom-left corner. You may want to copy this sign-in URL and open the console in a new window or browser.

   :image[accountaccess]{src="/static/accountaccess.png"}

You should now have access to your temporary AWS account. Note that this is a temporary account, which will be cleaned up when the event is terminated. In Workshop Studio, you should see a notice with the time remaining for the account.
  
 :image[eventtimer]{src="/static/eventtimer.png"}

 ::alert[For AWS events, this workshop is only provisioned to run in the US West (Oregon) region.]

::alert[Workshop Studio allows facilitators to federate into your account to help with any issues you might have. You will need to provide your account number to one of the facilitators to identify your account.]{header="Getting Help"}
